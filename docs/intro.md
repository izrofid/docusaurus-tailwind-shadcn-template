# What is Pokémon Emerald Imperium?

Pokémon Emerald Imperium is an expansive ROM hack built on top of pokeemerald-expansion. Drawing inspiration from modern difficulty hacks like Radical Red, it introduces a wealth of new content, mechanics, and quality-of-life enhancements while preserving the nostalgic charm of the original Hoenn journey. 

With 9 generations of Pokémon available to catch, improved trainer AI, enhanced difficulty, a dynamic level cap system, and countless other improvements — including a  brand-new custom Mega Evolutions. Emerald Imperium offers the new definitive way to experience the Hoenn region.
